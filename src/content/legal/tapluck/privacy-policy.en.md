## 1. Summary

TapLuck is an offline party game that picks a random player by detecting fingers
on the screen. **We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect
your name, email, or contacts.** The App works without you providing any personal
information. We do, however, use third-party services for advertising, purchases,
analytics and crash reporting, which process limited data as described below.

## 2. Data we process

We do not run our own servers that store your personal data. Data is processed by
the third-party SDKs embedded in the App:

| Purpose | Provider | Data processed |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising | Google AdMob | Advertising identifier, device/diagnostic data, approximate region, ad interaction. Used to serve and measure ads. |
| Purchases | RevenueCat + Apple/Google billing | Anonymous app user id, purchase/receipt status for the products you buy (e.g. remove ads, theme packs). No card data reaches us — payment is handled by Apple/Google. |
| Analytics | Google Firebase Analytics | Pseudonymous app instance id, in-app events (e.g. rounds played, theme selected), device model, OS, language. |
| Crash reporting | Firebase Crashlytics | Crash stack traces, device state at crash time, pseudonymous identifiers. |

On your device only, we store your preferences (selected theme, language, team
count, unlocked products) using local storage. This never leaves your device
except as part of a purchase record held by the billing/RevenueCat services.

For more on how these providers handle data, see their privacy policies:
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (AdMob, Firebase Analytics,
Crashlytics), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and
[RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## 3. Advertising and consent

- In the **European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland**, the App shows a Google
  UMP consent form before serving personalized ads. If you do not consent, ads
  are served in non-personalized mode.
- On **iOS 14.5+**, the App requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.
  If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used for cross-app tracking.
- You can reset or withdraw advertising consent at any time via your device
  settings, and (where offered) from within the App.

For details on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: **consent** (personalized advertising, analytics
where required), and **legitimate interests / contract** (delivering purchases
you request, basic functionality, security and crash diagnostics).

## 5. Your rights

Depending on your location (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA and similar), you may have the
right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, to object,
and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold directly-identifying data, please
contact us at [hello.diceandcode@gmail.com](mailto:hello.diceandcode@gmail.com)
and we will direct your request to the relevant processor. You may also exercise
rights directly with Google/Apple/RevenueCat.

## 6. Children's privacy

TapLuck is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 14
(the age of digital consent in Spain under the LOPDGDD). We do not knowingly
collect personal data from children under 14. If you believe a child has provided
us with personal data, contact us at
[hello.diceandcode@gmail.com](mailto:hello.diceandcode@gmail.com) and we will take
appropriate steps to delete it.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Local preferences persist until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Data held
by third-party processors is retained per their own policies (Google, Apple,
RevenueCat).

To delete the preferences stored on your device, uninstall the App or clear its
data in your device settings. For data held by the third-party processors, use
their own controls or contact us and we will forward your request.

## 8. International transfers

The third-party processors may process data outside your country, including the
United States, under their respective safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual
Clauses).
